Introduction

Hi there! I am Amolika Mehta, a 3rd year student of Bachelor of Science in Animation studying at the International School of Design in Pune. Welcome to my Autodesk Maya portfolio!

For the past several months, I have been working hard across the entire 3D pipeline, including modeling, texturing, lighting, rendering, dynamics and FX.

My absolute favourite part of this journey has been animation. Bringing movement to 3D models has been such an incredible experience.

Take a look around! If you have any questions or just want to connect, feel free to reach out. Thank you for visiting!
